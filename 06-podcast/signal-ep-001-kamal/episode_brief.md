## 1. Episode description

*~160 words — for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube description, show site.*

> In Episode 001 of The Signal, Trisden Mills and his co-founder Malik sit down with Kamal — an eleven-year recruitment veteran whose path runs from a biochemistry degree in his early twenties to recruiting software engineers across Europe for a major tech client.
>
> Kamal traces the arc honestly: the surprise of his son arriving, the cousin who pushed him into recruitment, the years at Frank Recruitment Group, the six-month solo agency that didn't work, and the move into RPO life. The conversation digs into what separates good recruiters from cowboys, why UK recruitment carries the stigma it does, and how AI and systems are quietly reshaping who survives the next decade.
>
> The centrepiece is Kamal's worst day — a story about a manager, a desk, and a turning point. The episode closes with a personal exchange on identity, fear, and doing the thing anyway.
>
> First in The Signal series.

## 3. Clip candidates

*Ten short-form moments to pull for LinkedIn / Instagram / TikTok. Each ~20–60 seconds. Listed in priority order.*

### Clip 1 — *"Fear is the killer of everything"*
> "Especially when it's something you're scared of. As soon as you start fearing that something is going to stop you, or fearing what people think — you won't do it. Everything is affected. Fear is the killer of everything. How you approach it, how you deliver — everything."

**Speaker:** Host · **Section:** What's next — and what stops you · **Why it lands:** Tight, universal, repeatable. Strongest mindset clip in the episode.

### Clip 2 — *The head-slamming desk story*
> "The big manager — five-foot-something, small guy — got up and started slamming his head off the desk. Full force. The last one was so hard he must have semi-passed out. Pointing at me, forehead red, semi-dazed: 'This is what you make me want to do.'"

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** The worst day · **Why it lands:** The episode's most cinematic moment. Pull the full ~60–90 seconds — setup, escalation, payoff.

### Clip 3 — *"10× more of the wrong thing"*
> "What I would have seen and heard is: 'Things are really bad. Do more of the same.' But if the solution is do ten times more of what we're doing — we're doing ten times more of the wrong thing."

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** Where it's going · **Why it lands:** Sharp business insight that translates straight to a recruiter's feed.

### Clip 4 — *"Selling shovels in a gold rush"*
> "We're at capacity because the space is so needed and everybody wants it. We're essentially selling shovels in a gold rush."

**Speaker:** Host · **Section:** Where it's going · **Why it lands:** Mentis-positioning soundbite. Strongest brand line in the episode.

### Clip 5 — *Cowboy recruiting — the 200/800 split*
> "Imagine you're a contractor, I'm your recruiter, I've placed you on a client site, and I'm making more money than you are as a day rate. I've seen madness. 200/800 splits. 300/700."

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** Top billers, integrity, and cowboy recruiting · **Why it lands:** Insider truth-telling. Recruiters and contractors will both share.

### Clip 6 — *"Don't compromise yourself"*
> "Don't compromise yourself. Don't let no one take you for a clown in this place. People take liberties because they've gotten away with it before."

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** The worst day · **Why it lands:** The lesson from the head-slam story. Pairs with Clip 2.

### Clip 7 — *The cold outreach*
> "Man came on the *coldest*. When I saw it, I laughed my head off. I was like — yeah, do you know what, this guy can get where he works."

**Speaker:** Host · **Section:** What's next — and what stops you · **Why it lands:** Meta moment about how the episode happened. Models cold outreach for the audience.

### Clip 8 — *Promotion-by-numbers myth*
> "Believing that someone is going to be a good leader because they were a good recruiter — that's a big myth in agencies. That's where a lot of agencies go wrong."

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** What recruiters get wrong · **Why it lands:** Quotable, contrarian, immediately useful for agency leaders.

### Clip 9 — *Quality over volume*
> "It's not about volume. It's about quality. Ten high-level conversations and three of them convert into business."

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** Some people can read off a sheet. Some can't. · **Why it lands:** Concrete principle, easy to caption, sales-floor relevance.

### Clip 10 — *The penny drops*
> "Some people will take six months to do their first deal. The first six months you really want it, and it's hard. But when you get to that first deal — they talk about it in recruitment, the *penny drops* — that's it."

**Speaker:** Kamal · **Section:** What broke you, what built you · **Why it lands:** Recognisable industry idiom; rookie recruiters will share with their cohort.
